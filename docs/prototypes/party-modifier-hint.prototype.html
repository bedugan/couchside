How do we say “hold RB first”?

The marks only work while the friendly targeting button is held, which is RB for you because your target modifiers are swapped. Each concept shows that differently. Press and hold Hold RB (or the R key) to see what happens while it's down. Press 1–3 or the bottom buttons to switch concepts.
