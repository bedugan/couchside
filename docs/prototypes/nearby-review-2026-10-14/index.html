What needs an in-game test

The 0.14.1 candidate fixes #2, #4, #5 and #7, and adds the panel report for #3. Local Lua checks do not establish controller safety. Brian has confirmed the requested test. The checklist below is retained as the test record; #3 needs the open-panel results before a guard change.

  1. First turn automatic scanning off with /cs option gathering.autoScan off, then reload the copied 0.14.1 candidate.
  2. Run /cs gathering panels with panels closed, then with bags, character, spellbook or a service panel open. Keep the panel open while issuing the command. Close each normally; check controller input.
  3. In a safe stationary spot, run /cs gathering scan. Check the list and close-pin marks. On a large minimap, confirm a scan can finish past twelve seconds without losing the result.
  4. Reload to persist the report. Check normal hover, movement, Jump and interaction after the scan and after reload.
  5. After the manual check, test automatic scanning while stationary for two minutes. This validates the new refresh lead together with the earlier once-per-stop behavior. Stop it with /cs gathering auto pause.

Do not open the settings panel in gamepad UI for this test. Use /cs option.
