What this provider does not resolve

Service exclusion, tracked-creature classification and creature-to-quest relationships still need their own evidence or data source. Name recognition does not prove native entity identity or improve the approximately 1,009-point scan by itself. Controller presentation, permanent herb meanings and observation freshness remain Couchside responsibilities.
